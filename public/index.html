Fishbowl

The party word game. One screen for the room, a phone for everyone.

How to play
  1. Open this page on a TV or laptop and choose Create a game.
  2. Everyone scans the QR code with their phone (or types the room code) and enters their name.
  3. Each player secretly adds a few words to the bowl — people, movies, places, anything.
  4. You're split into two teams. On your turn, your phone shows a word: get your team to guess as many as you can before time runs out.
  5. Three rounds, same words each time: Describe it, then Act it out, then One word. Most points wins!
